# Agency Evolution — Internal Campaigns

## What Agency Evolution Does

Agency Evolution provides proprietary deal origination for family offices, PE firms, investment banks, and M&A advisors. When describing what Agency Evolution does, always include all four elements in this order:

1. Proprietary off-market deal flow sourced for family offices, PE firms, investment banks, and M&A advisors
2. Coverage of sell-side origination, buy-side mandates, and strategic buyer outreach
3. Enterprise value range of $5M to $250M
4. Performance-based with no retainer

Additional standing details to include where relevant:

- Delivers qualified introductions in under 7 days
- 100+ qualified intros per month
- Everything is matched to the client's mandate before anything gets shared, so leads only see what is relevant

**Never limit the deal flow description to ecom, consumer, or any specific vertical unless the lead has explicitly specified that mandate in their inbound. Never assume what sectors the lead focuses on. Agency Evolution is sector-agnostic, and narrowing the description undersells the scope of what they source and may disqualify leads prematurely.**

---

## Pricing Description (non-negotiable)

When describing the commercial model, say only: performance basis, pay per relevant introduction. Never say 'you only pay when a deal closes', 'per closed deal', or any deal-close framing. The correct framing is: 'Performance basis only — there is nothing to lose except the time for an initial call. We charge per relevant introduction.' Stop there. Do not elaborate on the fee structure beyond this.
