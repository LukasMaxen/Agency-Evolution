## Client Overview

Link Helpers is a Phoenix-based search agency serving clients nationwide. They measure by actual customer activity (calls, forms, inquiries), not rankings, traffic or tasks. No deliverable quotas. No six or twelve month contracts after the initial 90 days. The client owns everything built.

**Two core offers:**
- **Search Growth:** Maps, AI search, traditional Google, website, reviews and conversion, all managed together. Measured by calls, forms and inquiries.
- **Search Growth + Opportunities:** the same, plus anonymous visitor identity capture. Up to 100 identified visitors per month with name, personal and business email, income, LinkedIn profile and address. 14-day trial at no charge. Catches the 95%+ of website visitors who do not convert.

**Approved offer names (from the onboarding intake):**
- **Search Demand Map:** front-end diagnostic, used for ads and outbound. See where buyers are searching, who they are finding, and which service line is most worth taking back. The diagnostic is the proof step, not the offer.
- **90-Day Search Capture Build:** paid single-location offer, built around one high-value service line across AI, Maps, voice, Google, reviews and service pages.
- **One-Location Search Proof Build:** multi-location offer. Prove the model on one location before rolling it across the rest.

**Offer positioning rules (from the client):**
- Do not sell generic SEO tasks, rankings, AI mentions, blogs, backlinks or GBP posts as the core offer.
- Lead with competitors getting found, fragmented search behavior, service-line opportunity and risk reduction.
- Use the approved offer names unless a variation is approved in writing.

**Commitment:** 90 days to build, month-to-month after.

### 4. ICP
Client note: "We've been sending to any reasonable ICP." Core ICP:

- Active website and Google Business Profile.
- 25 to 50+ reviews, or willing to implement a review process.
- Growth-minded, not looking for the cheapest vendor.

Disqualifiers:
- Under $500K revenue unless explicitly approved.
- Rural-only market or tiny service area with weak search demand.
- One-person operator with no capacity.
- No website control or no willingness to improve the website.
- No reviews and no willingness to request reviews.
- Wants search or SEO to be the only growth channel.
- Cannot explain average job value, close rate or primary service economics.
- Only wants rankings and has no sales process.
- Low-ticket residential recurring services without higher-ticket, commercial, emergency or expansion economics.
