# Agency Evolution — Company Overview

## What We Are

Agency Evolution is a **B2B cold outreach agency** specializing in lead generation for clients in the **Private Equity (PE) and Investment Banking (IB)** space. We run cold email campaigns on behalf of our clients and manage the full reply process — from inbox to booked call.

## Business Model

- We manage **15+ active client workspaces** simultaneously, each running their own cold outreach campaigns via EmailBison
- Our service covers: campaign setup, inbox management, AI-assisted reply handling, follow-up sequencing, and call booking
- Revenue comes from retainer relationships with clients — we are the outreach arm they don't build internally

## Core Service: Cold Email Outreach

- Clients hire us to target specific decision-makers (typically business owners, operators, or executives) with cold email sequences
- The goal for each client is to generate **booked discovery/acquisition calls** with qualified leads
- Most clients are in the deal-sourcing business (PE firms, acquisition entrepreneurs, capital advisors, franchisors)

## What We Operate

**AI Reply Desk** — a centralized internal dashboard built by us to:
- Aggregate inbound replies from all 16 EmailBison workspaces into a single inbox
- AI-analyze each reply for intent and urgency (via Claude Haiku)
- Draft and send replies on behalf of clients from one place
- Manage follow-up sequences (up to 10 FU emails per lead)
- Track call bookings via Calendly integration
- Surface meeting and activity data in a dashboard view

## Company Scale (as of April 2026)

- **15 active client workspaces** on a single EmailBison instance (`https://send.emailagencyevolution.com`)
- Internal tooling: Next.js 16 app deployed on Vercel, PostgreSQL on Hetzner
- No external auth — internal-only tool for the Agency Evolution team

## What Makes This Business Work

1. **Systemized reply handling** — templated replies + AI analysis means fast, consistent response times across all clients
2. **Centralized inbox** — one operator can manage replies across 15+ clients without switching tools
3. **Follow-up automation** — structured 10-step FU sequence ensures no lead is left behind
4. **Call booking integration** — Calendly links embedded directly in reply flow to reduce friction

## Positioning

Agency Evolution positions itself as a done-for-you outreach partner for firms that want deal flow or qualified meetings without building an internal SDR function. Clients range from solo acquisition entrepreneurs to capital advisory firms with specific deal mandates.
