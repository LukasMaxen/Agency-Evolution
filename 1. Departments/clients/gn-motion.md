## Offer

CGI product visualization. High-end animated and photorealistic product renders for consumer brands. Reference clients: Audemars Piguet, Starbucks, L'Oreal (Revitalift).

Hook: Complimentary CGI sample — always include, reason for sample differs by vertical.

## ICP

Consumer brand decision-makers across five verticals: Tech, Jewelry, Fashion, Cosmetics, Core (France). Marketing directors, brand managers, creative directors.

## Campaign Strategy

- Always lead with complimentary sample — it is the primary hook across all verticals
- Each vertical needs a different hook rationale:
  - Tech: product specs, hero shots, feature visualization
  - Jewelry: luxury presentation, light play, material texture
  - Fashion: lifestyle, movement, texture
  - Cosmetics: ingredient visualization, skin transformation, before/after (use L'Oreal Revitalift reference)
  - Core (France): leads in French, "Bien cordialement" closing
- Five active verticals running simultaneously

## Script Rules

- {FIRST_NAME} and {COMPANY} standard
- NO P.S. opt-out — consumer brand decision-makers, omit entirely
- French campaigns: write in French, close with "Bien cordialement"
- Use "complimentary" not "free"
- Match language of correspondence — French reply gets French email response
