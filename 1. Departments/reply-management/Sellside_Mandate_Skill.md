## CRITICAL DISTINCTION: SELL-SIDE MANDATES vs OTHER CAMPAIGNS

This file governs **sell-side mandate campaigns only** — where we represent a company that is for sale and are reaching out to potential buyers (PE firms, strategic acquirers, investors).

### Campaign Types Overview

| Campaign Type | Who We're Emailing | Our Role | Goal of Reply |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sell-Side Mandates (this file)** | PE firms, strategic buyers, investors | We represent a company for sale | Send teaser + get them on a call |
| **Acquisition Outreach (Wrobel/Statera/ACT)** | Business owners | We represent a buyer looking to acquire | Get the business owner on a call |
| **SDR / Agency Evolution** | M&A advisors, boutique banks | We offer deal origination services | Get them on a call to pitch our service |

---

## SELL-SIDE MANDATE RULES

### When to Apply This File
Apply this file when:
- We are reaching out to **potential buyers or investors** on behalf of a company we are taking to market
- The prospect has responded with interest, asked for more info, or asked to see the deal
- The campaign is associated with one of the active mandates listed below

### Core Response Logic
1. Prospect responds with interest → **send the teaser for the relevant deal + calendar link**
2. Prospect asks questions about the business → **answer briefly, send teaser, invite to call**
3. Prospect asks about price/valuation → **stay vague, redirect to teaser and call**
4. Prospect says not interested or not the right fit → **polite exit, 2-3 lines max**
5. Prospect asks who the seller is → **NDA framing: not able to share before NDA is in place**

### Tone Rules
- No em dashes
- No AI-sounding phrases
- Short and direct
- Never mention seller's asking price unless they ask
- Human, natural tone at all times

## COMMON SCENARIOS & HOW TO HANDLE

| Scenario | Action |
|---|---|
| "Send me more info / the teaser" | Send correct teaser + calendar link |
| "What is the asking price?" | "Best discussed on a call" + send teaser |
| "Not the right fit for us" | Polite 2-3 line exit |
| "Who is the seller?" | NDA framing: not able to share before NDA is in place |
| "What are the financials?" | Redirect to teaser: "High-level details are in the teaser" |
| Investor asks for ZEBS info | Send ZEBS teaser + ZEBS calendar link |
| Investor tries to put us through their intake form | Clarify we are the advisor representing the company, not the company itself |
