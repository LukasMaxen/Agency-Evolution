# Reply Management

**Owner:** Lukas Maxen

## What This Department Does

Handles everything that happens after a lead replies — AI analysis, reply drafting, objection handling, follow-up sequences, and call booking. Powered by our internal AI Reply Desk dashboard.

## Core Responsibilities

- Review and respond to inbound replies across all 15+ client workspaces
- Use AI-suggested replies as a starting point, edit as needed, and send
- Manage the 10-step follow-up sequence for interested leads who haven't booked
- Handle objections according to per-client guidelines
- Book discovery calls via Calendly or manual logging

## AI Can Help With

- Drafting replies based on lead intent and client tone
- Classifying reply intent (interested, needs info, not interested, unsubscribe)
- Generating follow-up emails for any step in the sequence
- Suggesting the right template based on objection type

## Workflow

```
Lead replies → AI Reply Desk → AI analyzes intent → Operator reviews + edits → Reply sent → Lead books call OR follow-up sequence starts
```
