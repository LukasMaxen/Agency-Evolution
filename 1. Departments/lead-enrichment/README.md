# Lead Enrichment

**Owner:** Sunny Newar

## What This Department Does

Takes raw contact lists from lead sourcing and enriches them with the additional data needed to run effective cold email campaigns — verified emails, company details, personalisation data, and quality scores.

## Core Responsibilities

- Verify and validate email addresses (reduce bounce rates)
- Enrich contacts with job title, company size, revenue, and other relevant data
- Score and prioritise leads before passing to campaign management
- Maintain data quality standards across all client lists

## AI Can Help With

- Writing enrichment prompts for tools like Clay or Apollo
- Scoring leads based on ICP fit criteria
- Generating personalisation snippets from enriched data
- Identifying missing data fields and suggesting sources

## Workflow

```
Raw list (from lead-sourcing/) → Email verification → Data enrichment → Lead scoring → Hand off to campaign-management/
```
