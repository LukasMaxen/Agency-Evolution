## The Vision

Maxen Partners eliminates "Referral Reliance" for M&A advisors and Private Equity firms by deploying a high-volume, proprietary origination system. We identify, vet, and deliver off-market opportunities directly into your pipeline, allowing you to focus exclusively on closing.

---

## Who We Serve (ICP)

**Two primary client pillars:**

1. **Solo/Boutique M&A Intermediaries** — advisors and boutique firms who need consistent deal flow without building an internal sourcing team
2. **Direct Private Equity Buyers** — PE firms and family offices that want off-market seller introductions matched to their specific mandate

**Note on legacy clients:** We still service clients in other verticals (e.g. 911 Restoration franchise, GN Motion CGI, Sonaro AI, ITG Group) from earlier agency work. These remain active in the `clients/` folder. We are not signing new clients in these categories — the rebrand and new ICP is focused exclusively on M&A intermediaries and direct PE/strategic buyers.

## Pain Points We Solve

| Pain Point | Description |
|---|---|
| **The "Zero-Year" Risk** | Revenue tied to 1–3 unpredictable referrals creates dangerous volatility |
| **The "Manual Grind" Trap** | Senior partners spending 40+ hours/week on low-leverage cold outreach instead of deal execution |
| **Fragmented Data** | No centralized system to track, nurture, and own long-tail seller relationships |
| **Hidden Opportunity Costs** | Missing $400k+ commission opportunities because the firm lacks infrastructure to reach thousands of founders monthly |

---

## What We Do (The Solution)

Maxen Partners provides a full-funnel origination service that handles everything from initial data procurement to warm introduction.

| Service | Description |
|---|---|
| **Proprietary Sourcing** | High-volume, multi-channel outbound campaigns (email, LinkedIn, cold call) generating 20–50 sellers per month |
| **Founder Vetting** | We verify interest and initial financials (Revenue/EBITDA) before any handoff occurs — not just "leads" |
| **Asset Creation (CRM)** | We build and manage a custom Airtable CRM for every client, ensuring they own 100% of the proprietary data |
| **Buyer Sourcing Support** | For intermediaries, we source the PE/strategic buyer once the sell-side mandate is signed |

---

## How We Do It (The Method)

**Section 1 — Mandate Alignment**
Deep-dive into the client's specific sectors, EBITDA targets, and geographic mandates.

**Section 2 — High-Volume Infrastructure**
Deploy high-quality, high-volume outreach across email, LinkedIn, and cold calling.

**Section 3 — The Handoff**
- For PE partners: provide "Blind Teasers" so they can green-light a lead before introduction is made (protects professional credibility)
- For intermediary clients: source leads directly within their own business

## Positioning Notes for AI

When writing anything in the voice of Maxen Partners (e.g. our own website copy, pitch decks, LinkedIn content, or internal positioning):

- Lead with **deal flow predictability** — this is the core pain we solve
- Use "off-market" as a key differentiator phrase
- Reference "referral reliance" as the problem we eliminate
- Always position us as a **system**, not a service — our clients get infrastructure, not just leads
- For M&A clients: emphasize the **blind teaser / vetting** step — this is what makes us credible in the M&A world
- Never position us as a generic "lead gen" agency — we are an origination system
