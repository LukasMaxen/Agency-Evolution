# Lead Sourcing

**Owners:** Sunny Newar + Kasper Zacho

## What This Department Does

Builds the raw contact lists that fuel our cold email campaigns. Every client engagement starts here — without qualified leads, there are no emails to send.

## Core Responsibilities

- Define the Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) for each client
- Source contact lists from databases, LinkedIn, and other channels
- Verify list quality before handing off to lead enrichment
- Maintain sourcing standards across all 15+ client workspaces

## AI Can Help With

- Generating ICP definitions based on client brief
- Writing Boolean search strings for LinkedIn/Apollo/Clay
- Scoring and filtering raw lead lists
- Identifying sourcing gaps or patterns across clients

## Workflow

```
Client brief → ICP definition → Source lists → Quality check → Hand off to lead-enrichment/
```
