## What We Are

[COMPANY NAME] is a **buy-side sourcing partner** for firms in the Private Equity (PE) and Investment Banking (IB) space. We run cold email outreach campaigns on behalf of our clients and manage the full reply process — from inbox to booked discovery/acquisition call.

## Business Model

- We manage 15+ active client workspaces simultaneously, each running their own outreach campaigns via EmailBison
- Our service covers: campaign setup, inbox management, AI-assisted reply handling, follow-up sequencing, and call booking
- Revenue comes from retainer relationships — we are the outreach arm clients don't build internally

## Core Service: Outbound Sourcing

- Clients hire us to reach specific decision-makers (business owners, operators, executives) with targeted cold email sequences
- The goal for each client is to generate **booked discovery or acquisition calls** with qualified leads
- Most clients are in the deal-sourcing business (PE firms, acquisition entrepreneurs, capital advisors, franchisors)

## What Makes This Work

1. **Systemized reply handling** — templated replies + AI analysis = fast, consistent responses across all clients
2. **Centralised inbox** — one operator manages replies across 15+ clients without switching tools
3. **Follow-up automation** — structured 10-step FU sequence ensures no lead is left behind
4. **Call booking integration** — Calendly links embedded directly in the reply flow

## Internal Tooling: AI Reply Desk

A centralised dashboard built by the team to:
- Aggregate inbound replies from all workspaces into a single inbox
- AI-analyze each reply for intent and urgency (Claude Haiku)
- Draft and send replies on behalf of clients from one place
- Manage follow-up sequences and track call bookings
