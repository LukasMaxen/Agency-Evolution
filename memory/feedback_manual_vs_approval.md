When an interested lead proposes a specific time window ("next week", "around next week", "Monday", "tomorrow", "next Tuesday", etc.), that goes to #manual-replies for the team to book manually. It does NOT go to #reply-approval.

Reply-approval is only for general interest with no time given ("happy to chat", "tell me more", "send the teaser", "sounds interesting").

**Why:** The lead has already said when they want to meet. Sending a drafted reply asking to book is wrong — the team needs to open the calendar and place the meeting.

**How to apply:** Before routing any interested reply to reply-approval, check if the lead named a time window. If yes, flag as manual and post to #manual-replies.
