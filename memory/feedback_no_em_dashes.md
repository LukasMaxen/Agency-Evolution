Never use em dashes (—), en dashes (–), or any dash used as punctuation in any output. Avoid dashes in general across all written content.

**Why:** Explicit instruction from Kasper. Dashes of any kind look unnatural in outreach copy and should not appear anywhere.

**How to apply:** Restructure the sentence, use a comma, period, or colon instead. No exceptions. Applies to reply drafts, FU sequences, Slack messages, analysis, all output.
