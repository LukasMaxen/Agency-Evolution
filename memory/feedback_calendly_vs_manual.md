**All clients:** When a lead asks for availability, wants to book a call, or confirms interest in speaking, include the Calendly link from the client file in the reply. Do not post to #manual-replies for scheduling.

**Why:** We have Calendly links for all clients in their client files. The old rule of routing all non-Larsen Digital leads to #manual-replies was changed on 2026-05-06.

**How to apply:** Read the client file, find the correct Calendly link for the sender, and include it in the reply. Match the link to the right campaign track if the client has multiple (e.g. Statera has a separate M&A and ZEBS link).
