# Sonaro AI – Outreach & Reply Handling Guidelines

## Handling Neutral Replies

When a lead's reply is neutral — such as a correction, clarification, or factual update with no clear buying signal — do not default to a hard meeting or call CTA. The lead has not yet indicated interest, and pushing for a scheduled call at this stage can feel presumptuous.

Instead, use a softer, exploratory CTA that mirrors the tone of the initial outreach email. For example, ask whether it would be worth a quick chat, rather than directly requesting to book a call.

**Why this matters:**
- A neutral reply is not a buying signal — it's an opening, not a commitment.
- Mirroring the low-pressure tone of the first email keeps the conversation natural and avoids friction.
- A softer CTA preserves optionality and lets the lead self-select into the next step.

**Example of what to avoid:**
> "Would you be open to a 20-minute call this week to discuss further?"

**Example of a preferred CTA in this context:**
> "Would it be worth a quick chat to see if there's anything relevant here for you?"

This approach applies across reply-handling workflows where the lead's intent has not yet been clearly established.
