## Personalization Guidelines

### Structure Rule (from Kasper Zacho send-time learning)
Always lead with the **value proposition first**, then earn the personalization second:

1. **What 911 Restoration offers** — insurance pipelines, no cold outreach, existing business keeps running
2. **Why their specific profile fits** — compliment lands *after* the concept is established, not upfront

This framing feels less like a pitch built around them and more like a genuine explanation that earns the personalization.

### {PERSONALIZATION_LINE} Examples
Fill this token with a specific, research-backed detail about the lead's existing business or background. The personalization should feel earned and specific — never generic.

- **Service business with recurring clients:** "you already understand retention and repeat revenue, which translates directly"
- **Owner with staff/team:** "managing a team means the operational side of a restoration crew won't be new territory"
- **Wellness/home services crossover:** "your client base already trusts you inside their homes and their routines"
- **Multi-location or growth-minded operator:** "you've already scaled once, which is exactly the profile that accelerates fastest in our network"
- **Solo operator or lean business:** "you've built real revenue with a lean operation — that discipline is an asset here"
